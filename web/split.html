👥 Local Play on this device

← Back to the emulator
Choose a game. Each player gets a screen of their own.
Every screen is its own console with its own address, linked to the others through memory shared on this device instead of a network, so use the game's local wireless (Local Play / Download Play) menus as on real consoles. Click a screen, or press Ctrl+1–4, to give it the keyboard; controllers go to the players in the order they connect. All consoles share this device's processor, so a demanding game runs slower than on its own.
