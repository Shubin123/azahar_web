👥 Local Play on this device

← Back to the emulator
Choose a game. Each player gets a screen of their own.
Every screen is its own console with its own address, linked to the others through memory shared on this device instead of a network, so use the game's local wireless (Local Play / Download Play) menus as on real consoles: host a room on one screen and join it from the others. A Download Play room is joined with 📥 on the other screens (each uses the Download Play part of its own copy of the game). ↺ restarts one console afresh while the others play on; ⤺ brings every console back to the checkpoint they last took together, so their games agree again. A console that stops is restarted on its own, and everyone goes back to the checkpoint when there is one. Players 1 and 2 share the keyboard, each with their own keys (listed under each screen, or pick other keys there); Ctrl+1–9 gives one player the whole keyboard and Ctrl+0 shares it again. Controllers go to the players in the order they connect, or pick one per screen; click a screen to touch it with the mouse. All consoles share this device's processor, so a demanding game runs slower than on its own.

How Player 2 joins a game room

Every screen is its own console. One player hosts a room in the game; the others join it. How they join depends on the game.

Download Play games (such as Mario Party: Island Tour)

  1. On Player 1's screen, open the game's multiplayer mode (Mario Party: Multiplayer). Its lobby says it is gathering players.
  2. Leave the other screens on the game's title screen: if two screens open the lobby, both are hosting and nobody can join.
  3. On Player 2's screen, click 📥 Join Player 1's Download Play at its bottom right. It shows while Player 1's lobby is open and Player 2 is in no room. Do the same on every other screen that should play.
  4. Within a few seconds each player is listed in Player 1's lobby.
  5. Player 1 starts the game (Mario Party: End). The other screens restart into the game's Download Play version and join Player 1's game.

Games where everyone has the game (such as Mario Kart 7)

  1. Player 1 hosts from the game's own menu (Mario Kart 7: Local Multiplayer, then create a room).
  2. Player 2 opens the same menu on their screen and picks Player 1's room from the list.

Some of these games accept Download Play players too (Mario Kart 7 does), so 📥 can show there as well; joining from the game's menu plays the full game.

Players on other devices or browsers

  1. On the emulator page, link the consoles in the 📡 Local Play card: send an invite code and paste back the reply, or use a public lobby.
  2. Join from the game's menu as above. For a Download Play room, press 📥 Join Download Play in the Local Play card: it joins the first room offering the game.

When joining does not work

  • The bar above each screen says which room its console is in: 🏠 Hosting a room · 2 players on the host, 🚪 In Player 1's room on a player who joined.
  • If a screen stays on 📥 Joining… (the host left its lobby, say), press ↺ on that screen and join again once the lobby is open.
  • ↺ restarts one console; ⤺ Back to checkpoint brings every console back to the moment they last saved together, still in one room.
